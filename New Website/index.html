Your hardest documents are our speciality

Generic OCR and off-the-shelf AI tools fail on the documents that matter most — scanned project appraisals from the 1990s, faxed compliance reports, multilingual evaluation documents with inconsistent formatting, and portfolio reports where every company uses a different template.

Our multi-agent system uses specialized vision models, context-aware extraction, and confidence scoring to handle documents that other tools simply cannot process — including poor-quality scans and handwritten annotations. Every output is audit-ready with full provenance tracking.
