    Case studies

    Destillo grew out of years spent building machine learning and AI systems inside multilateral development banks, including the World Bank Group and the Asian Development Bank. Five of those engagements are below, each run on real institutional material at portfolio scale.

    Asian Development Bank · IEDM&E quality

    Quality of M&E systems across small island developing states

    Assessing how well monitoring and evaluation systems were designed and used across operations in small island developing states, where thin statistical capacity and small sample sizes make results reporting hardest to verify.

    Twenty evaluation questions were applied to every page of the corpus. Baseline presence, indicator measurability, data source specification, endline collection and sixteen others. The result is a comparable assessment per project rather than a narrative impression across the group.

    World Bank Group · IEGEvaluation

    Guarantee instrument evaluation

    An evaluation of the guarantee portfolio required consistent extraction from a document set spanning instruments, regions and two decades of changing templates. Twenty-seven distinct questions were applied across all 25,000 pages, with no sampling.

    The methodology was benchmarked head to head against manual review of the same documents. The evaluation team received portfolio-wide coverage, plus exact figures on where the pipeline agreed with reviewers and where it did not. That reporting is what makes an accuracy claim usable rather than promotional.

    Finding digital projects

    Digital work is rarely labelled as such. It sits inside transport, health and public sector operations as a component, so the portfolio cannot be identified from project titles or sector codes alone.

    All 5,500 documents were classified for whether the project belonged in the digital portfolio, with the decision and its basis recorded per document, so the inclusion boundary could be reviewed and defended rather than assumed.

    World Bank GroupReplicability

    Replications across MDBs

    A study of replicability across development finance portfolios: which project designs recur, which correlate with results, and which are specific to their context.

    Ten thousand projects were assembled from more than ten data sources across five institutions, including EBRD. Embedding-based similarity identified comparable operations across institutional boundaries, and surfaced correlations that keyword matching on five different taxonomies could not.

    World Bank Group · GFDRRCompliance · Multilingual

    Building compliance assessed against a structural checklist

    Building documentation had to be assessed against a structural safety and compliance checklist, across a document set filed in several languages. More than fifty structural questions were built into the pipeline, each returning an assessment traceable to the drawing, specification or report clause it came from.

    Translation was built into the pipeline rather than bolted on. Non-English submissions were assessed against the same checklist as English ones, and the citation kept pointing at the original text. This is the engagement the multilingual capability came out of.
